# UPSTREAM-POLICY-QUESTIONS.md

These questions were discovered during ES-LATAM adaptation work and cannot be solved through translation alone.

They should not be silently harmonized in Spanish.

## Open questions

1. **What does `Reduced expectations when needed` reduce?**  
   Participation, workload, deadlines, learning outcomes, conduct expectations, or something else?

2. **Is documentation required for accommodations?**  
   If yes, when, what may facilitators request, and what disclosure remains voluntary?

3. **What is the governing appeals policy?**  
   The official CoC says `no appeals`; `part4/removal-reentry.md` contains an appeals process.

4. **How do progressive discipline and immediate/final removal authority interact?**  
   What behaviors bypass warnings, and what procedural minimum is guaranteed?

5. **What triggers the `new rule` expulsion clause?**  
   Is the need for a new rule itself sufficient for expulsion without refund?

6. **What is the operational scope of `No cops`?**  
   What counts as acting like a cop, and what are the safety/emergency exceptions?

7. **What does `gender griefing` mean behaviorally?**  
   It appears in the official CoC as a sanction-bearing label without a clear behavioral definition.
