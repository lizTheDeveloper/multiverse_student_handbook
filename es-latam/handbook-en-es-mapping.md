## Purpose and control rules

This document inventories the bridge between the English Handbook and the provisional ES-LATAM terminology evidence base. It does not translate the Handbook and does not approve final Spanish canon.

The English files control substantive Handbook claims and institutional policy. `glosario-base-es-latam-v3.md` controls only the available Spanish terminology evidence. A gap or disagreement in the glossary must not silently delete, soften, medically validate, or rewrite an English source claim. Such cases are isolated for source/editorial review.

Mapping statuses:

- `DIRECT`: the Spanish evidence base supports a materially equivalent working term.
- `CONTEXTUAL`: Spanish evidence exists, but the choice depends on identity, function, register, or local sentence context.
- `MAPPING-GAP`: the Handbook needs a Spanish term, but the supplied Spanish corpus does not support a sufficient mapping.
- `SOURCE-CLAIM-OUTSIDE-CORPUS`: the English source makes a substantive framing or clinical claim that the Spanish corpus does not establish. Preserve it as an English-source claim unless editorial review changes the source content.
- `POLICY-SENSITIVE`: wording affects institutional rights, duties, enforcement, safety, privacy, or commitments and cannot be settled as terminology alone.
- `US-JURISDICTION`: the item is tied to United States law, benefits, agencies, services, phone numbers, or procedures and requires explicit jurisdictional labeling rather than simple localization.

| 4 | `part7/borderline-personality-disorder.md` | Forces the workflow to demonstrate that it can preserve an English source claim while refusing to misrepresent that claim as Spanish-corpus consensus. | SOURCE-CLAIM-OUTSIDE-CORPUS, CONTEXTUAL, MAPPING-GAP, POLICY-SENSITIVE |

### Pilot method

1. Freeze the four English files at `9e6a140e28b5c27e8cc51536bc3021d61f116af6` and retain path/section anchors in the working translation.
2. Resolve or explicitly mark HD-01 through HD-07 before treating policy wording as publishable. Other human-decision items may remain visible annotations during the pilot.
3. For each specialized English occurrence, carry forward the A-section status and record the actual Spanish choice; do not promote a contextual choice to project-wide canon merely because it worked once.
4. Keep Multiverse commitments separate from external examples and U.S. rights/resources. Do not convert WAZÚ, UV, SENADIS or UNLP recommendations into school promises.
5. In the BPD pilot, preserve claim attribution and attach `SOURCE-CLAIM-OUTSIDE-CORPUS` wherever the English source exceeds the Spanish evidence base. Do not fill clinical vocabulary from general knowledge.
6. After the four-file pass, apply the same source-claim test to `part7/schizophrenia-psychosis.md` before translating the remaining mental-health and crisis cluster.

### Pilot acceptance criteria

- Every specialized occurrence is traceable to an English path/section and one of the six mapping statuses.
- `accommodation`, `adjustment` and `support` are mapped by function, not by a global word substitution.
- The four verified accommodation commitments and three explicit limits remain intact and no external recommendation becomes a Multiverse promise.
- Autism identity language follows the evidence while individual self-description remains possible.
- BPD material is presented as Handbook source content, not as consensus of the Spanish terminology corpus.
- U.S. laws, benefits, agencies and crisis services are visibly labeled as U.S.-jurisdictional.
- Policy contradictions and undecided coined terms remain visible rather than silently harmonized.
- `part3/suicidal-students.md` remains recorded as an upstream broken link. No other file is substituted for it.

### Pilot boundary

This pilot should not be treated as the final Spanish Handbook, final canon or a factual verification of medical/legal/resource claims. It tests whether the adaptation workflow can preserve policy, provenance, source claims and evidence gaps without flattening them into a fluent but inaccurate translation.
