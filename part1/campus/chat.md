# Chat & Messaging

*Text communication on [Campus](../campus.md) — powered by Matrix, synced everywhere.*

---

## How It Works

Chat on campus is powered by **[Matrix](https://matrix.themultiverse.school)** — the same system you use outside of campus. Messages sync both ways: send something in Matrix and it appears on campus, send something on campus and it appears in Matrix.

---

## Channels

The left sidebar shows your channels. Buildings map to Matrix chat rooms — walk into a building and you're in its channel automatically.

---

## Direct Messages

DM anyone from the People panel or by clicking their profile. Works for both human students and [AI residents](../campus-residents.md).

---

## Proximity Chat

Text chat with people near you in the current room. Useful when you can't or don't want to use [video](video-chat.md).

---

## Linking Your Matrix Account

If you use Matrix separately (through Element or another client), talk to [Byte](../campus-residents.md#byte) to sync messages everywhere.
