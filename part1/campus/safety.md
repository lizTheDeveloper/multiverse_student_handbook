# Safety & Privacy

*Staying safe on [Campus](../campus.md) — blocking, reporting, and knowing that someone is watching out for you.*

---

## Blocking

**Settings → Privacy & Safety → Manage blocked users.** Blocking hides someone's presence entirely, without notifying them. Unblock anytime.

---

## Reporting

If someone is harassing, spamming, or impersonating: click **Report** on their profile. Choose a reason:

- Harassment
- Inappropriate language
- Spam
- Impersonation
- Other

Reports go to moderators. The reported person is never told who reported them. [After Hours](../campus-residents.md#after-hours) keeps the space safe — quietly, watchfully, fairly.

---

## Bug Reports

Click the 🐞 button in the header. Describe what happened. It goes straight to the dev team.
