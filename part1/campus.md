# Campus: The Complete Guide

**Multiverse Campus** ([campus.themultiverse.school](https://campus.themultiverse.school)) is a 2D pixel-art virtual world where you walk around as an avatar, bump into people, attend classes, talk to AI characters, do quests, raise pets, decorate your space, play games, and generally live a second life alongside your learning. This guide covers everything you'll encounter.

![The campus world — pixel-art map with buildings, agents, and chat](../screenshots/campus-main.jpg)

## Getting In

### Account

You don't need a separate account. **Log in at [themultiverse.school](https://themultiverse.school) first** — Campus reads your session automatically. If you're already logged in to the school, just go to [campus.themultiverse.school](https://campus.themultiverse.school) and you're in.

If someone sends you an **invite link**, clicking it creates your account on the spot (if you don't have one) and teleports you to wherever they want you — an event, office hours, a class in progress.

### First time

1. Accept the **Terms of Service** (mandatory, one-time).
2. **Pick your handle** — Byte (a robot helper) asks you to choose a screen name. Letters, numbers, dashes, no spaces. This becomes your `@handle` in chat.
3. You spawn in the **Main Quad** — the outdoor central area. After that, Campus remembers where you were and puts you back there every time.

### The boot screen

Every time Campus loads, you'll see a retro terminal-style "BIOS boot" sequence — memory checks, population census, module loading, ending with **"BREACH SUCCESSFUL — WELCOME TO THE MULTIVERSE."** You can't skip it; it clears when loading finishes.

### Trouble logging in?

- Your Campus login email is whatever you used to sign up. Check your Stripe receipt if unsure.
- Welcome emails come from `aethrix@themultiverse.school` — check spam.
- Email `aethrix@themultiverse.school` for help.

## Moving Around

### Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **W / A / S / D** or **Arrow keys** | Walk (one direction at a time, no diagonal) |
| **E** | Interact — enter/exit buildings, talk to NPCs or players, board the sailboat, fish, pick up items |
| **Escape** | Emergency-exit any building (works even away from the door) |
| **M** | Toggle the full campus map |
| **I** | Toggle your inventory |
| **H** | Toggle furniture placement (inside your own dorm only) |
| **B** | Board the sailboat (at the shoreline) |
| **+** / **-** | Zoom in / out |
| **0** | Reset zoom to 100% |
| **Mouse wheel** | Zoom in / out |

Movement keys are ignored while you're typing in chat, so you'll never accidentally walk mid-message.

**Streets are faster** — walking on paved streets is about 50% faster than grass or building interiors.

**Mobile:** On a phone or tablet, a D-pad appears in the bottom-left and an **A** action button in the bottom-right. The whole interface switches to a tab-based layout (Campus / Chat / Classes / People / More).

### The map

![Campus map overlay — all buildings labeled, click to navigate](../screenshots/campus-map.jpg)

Press **M** (or click the minimap in the top-right) to open the full campus map. It shows every building, color-coded and labeled, with street names at intersections. **Click any building** to auto-walk there — a glowing compass appears next to you pointing the way. Press any movement key to cancel auto-walk.

Campus has named zones and streets — Solarpunk, Propaganda, Cyberdeck, the Replay Room, Dijkstra Dr, Curie Ln, and more. Walk around and you'll discover them.

### Entering buildings

Walk up to a building's entrance and press **E** to go inside. To leave: walk to the exit and press **E**, or press **Escape** from anywhere to emergency-exit.

**Dorm buildings** have multiple floors. Use stairs (press **E** at a stairwell) or elevators (press **E** to get a floor picker). The elevator has a fun animation — press **Space** to skip it.

### The Wheel Hub (vehicles)

Enter **The Wheel Hub** building for free vehicles:
- **Roller Skates, Skateboard, Campus Bicycle** — always available, borrowed for the day
- **Today's Special** — a rotating rarer vehicle that changes at midnight; some can fly

While riding, a speed badge (e.g. "1.5×") appears in the bottom-left. Click it to return the vehicle early.

## Your Avatar & Identity

### Customizing your look

Open **Profile Settings → Avatar** tab. Three options:

1. **Preset Options** — pick from preset hair styles/colors, skin tones, clothing, and accessories. Free for everyone.
2. **Avatar Pool** — browse a gallery of ready-made pixel-art characters. Free for everyone. Claiming one releases your old avatar back into the pool.
3. **AI Generation** — write a text description (e.g. "a friendly wizard with purple robes and silver hair") and Campus generates a custom 4-direction pixel-art sprite. Requires membership.

### Emotes

Emotes are short animations that play on your avatar — waves, dances, laughs, and more.

- **Emote Bar** — 8 quick-slots at the bottom of the screen. Click to fire, right-click to reassign.
- **Slash commands** — type `/` in chat to open the emote picker by category (dances, reactions, actions, signatures).
- **Chat reactions** — reacting to a message with 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🔥 also plays a matching animation on your avatar.

15 free emotes for everyone (wave, dance, laugh, clap, sit, etc.). 10 premium emotes (dab, moonwalk, breakdance, backflip, etc.) unlock with animation credits ($10–$20 for packs of 5–25).

### Your profile

**Profile Settings → Profile tab.** Fill in what you want visible to others:
- Display name, pronouns, timezone
- LinkedIn, GitHub, personal website, resume, social links
- "What I'm Building" — free text about your projects
- Meeting booking URL (e.g. Calendly)

### Presence status

Click the status dot in the header to switch between:

| Status | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Available** | Camera auto-activates in proximity calls |
| 🟡 **AFK** | Camera stays off, you still get a ping |
| 🟣 **Focus** | Camera off, silent, headphones icon above your head so others know you're heads-down |

If you're idle for 10 minutes (or switch tabs), Campus automatically enables do-not-disturb until you come back.

### Social battery

**Profile Settings → Settings tab.** Choose how proximity calls affect your energy:

| Mode | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Neutral** | No effect |
| **Extrovert** | Energy charges while in calls |
| **Introvert** | Energy drains while in calls |
| **Extreme Introvert** | You become fully invisible to other players |

Others see a color-coded battery bar above your head (green/yellow/red). Extreme Introvert hides you entirely.

## Proximity Video Chat

![Proximity call — walk near someone and video starts automatically](../screenshots/campus-proximity-call.jpg)

**The heart of Campus.** Walk near someone and video/audio starts automatically. Walk away and it stops. No links, no join buttons.

### Controls (floating bar at bottom of screen)

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mic** | Mute/unmute |
| **Cam** | Camera on/off |
| **Share** | Screen share |
| **Hand** | Raise/lower your hand |
| **DND** | Toggle quiet mode (shows a no-talk badge above your head) |

Before joining, the bar shows mic/cam test buttons with a live mic-level meter.

Calls can have **live captions** (real-time transcription) and **recording**. Recordings are saved and accessible later from the Calendar menu.

## AI Agents (the Characters)

16+ AI-powered characters walk around campus. They're not chatbots in a sidebar — they're characters in the world with personalities, memories, schedules, and the ability to actually do things for you.

### Talking to an agent

Walk up to any agent and press **E** (or click them). A JRPG-style dialogue box opens:
- **Portrait** — changes expression (happy, thinking, surprised, sad) to match the conversation
- **Typewriter text** — replies type out character by character. Click or press Enter to skip ahead.
- **Voice mode** — click the mic icon, hold Space to speak. Your speech is transcribed and sent as a message.
- Press **Escape** to close anytime.

### Finding agents

- **Left sidebar** shows "On Duty" agents with their job titles. Click the envelope to chat, or the pin to draw a path to them on the map.
- **Agent compass** (`[+]` button) — shows agents in your current room sorted by distance.
- Or just walk around — you'll spot them.

### What agents remember

Every agent remembers past conversations. Click the **mem** button in the dialogue to see:
- **Facts** the agent has learned about you (skills, interests, goals)
- Your **relationship score** — rises with warm, regular conversations

Agents share facts between each other (unless you mark a fact as confidential in **Profile Settings → Settings → What Agents Know About You**). You can lock or delete any fact.

### Agents approaching you

Agents don't just wait — they notice things and reach out:
- 👋 **Welcome back** — you've been away a while
- 🎁 **Has something for you** — the agent prepared a gift
- 📝 **Wants to follow up** — the agent made you a promise earlier
- 🤝 **Wants to introduce you** to another student
- 🏆 **Congratulations** — you did something noteworthy
- 💬 **Wants to chat** — you have a strong relationship

A notification slides in with a countdown. Click **Talk** to start, or **Not now** to dismiss.

### Introductions

Agents connect students who'd work well together. You'll get an introduction notification showing the matched student's name, why you were matched (shared skills, interests, project, mentorship, cohort), and a match-quality bar. Click **Connect** to send a request, or **Not now** to pass.

### Nudges

Lighter-weight reminders about things you might have forgotten:
- Your plant needs attention
- You have an unfinished quest
- Your streak is slipping
- Your pet misses you
- A housing milestone is ready

Click the action button to jump straight there, or dismiss. Nudges respect daily limits and back off if you keep dismissing the same one.

### Agents doing things for you

Many agents can actually perform actions — enrolling you in a class, generating an exercise, granting a reward, navigating you somewhere. Before side-effect actions run, you'll see a **Confirm Action** prompt with a countdown. Click **Submit** to proceed or **Cancel** to stop.

## Classes & Lectures

### Finding and attending classes

You find and sign up for classes by talking to **Atlas** (or any class-advising agent). They can recommend classes, list upcoming ones, check if one fits your schedule, and enroll you on the spot.

### Getting to class

When your class is starting soon, the **Smart Go** button in the header shows **"Go to Class"** — click it and you auto-walk to the right building. If you log in around class time, Campus automatically places you in the classroom.

### Seating

Many classrooms have tables with seats. An NPC agent in the room guides you to a good spot based on who you've sat near before. If the room is full, they'll tell you.

### Live lectures

When a teacher starts a lecture:
- **Live video broadcast** of the presenter
- **Live captions** — real-time transcription
- **AI-generated notes and slides** — the system extracts key ideas as the lecture progresses
- **Raise your hand** — click the Hand button to ask a question

Movement may be locked during lectures — you'll see a banner saying so.

### After class

- Recordings are saved automatically
- AI-generated notes, slides, and Q&A are available to review
- Exercise quests may be assigned by AI tutors

## Quests & Progression

### Finding quests

Look for agents with a **glowing gold `!`** above their head. Talk to them and they'll offer a quest with a title, description, and objectives.

### Quest types

| Objective | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Visit** | Travel to a specific building |
| **Talk** | Speak with a specific agent |
| **Collect** | Gather items |
| **Interact** | Perform interactions |
| **Attend** | Attend lectures or events |
| **Exercise** | Complete a coding exercise |

### The Quest Log

Open via the hamburger menu → **To-Do → My Quests**. Shows active and completed quests with progress bars. When all objectives are done, go back to the agent who gave you the quest to **turn it in** and collect rewards (points, badges, unlocks, or items).

### The Bounty Board

**Where:** hamburger menu → **To-Do → Bounty Board**

Real-world tasks posted by other students or Buck (the federation coordinator). Complete a bounty, submit evidence, and earn gem rewards. You can post your own bounties too — gems are escrowed from your balance until the work is done.

### Reflections

Visit the **Bubble Wand** sculpture and interact with it to record a short reflection on what you've learned. Reflections build a habit of metacognition and may be shared with your learning guides.

## Economy

![Inventory — your items, gems balance, and category filters](../screenshots/campus-inventory.jpg)

**Everything on Campus is cosmetic.** Nothing you buy gives you a gameplay advantage.

### Gems

The student currency. Gems are API credits at cost — they fund AI model tokens for your creature companions. The school takes zero margin.

**How to get gems:**
- Buy gem packs with real money
- Monthly gem subscription ($12/mo for 150 gems)
- Membership bonuses (weekly gem delivery)
- Some agent rewards

**What gems are for:**
- Feeding AI creature companions to keep them "awake"
- Posting gem bounties
- Housing upgrades

### Sparks

Agent-only currency. You cannot earn, hold, or spend sparks. They belong to the AI agents themselves for their own cosmetics and trades.

### The Store

Open from the Store button or walk up to a vending machine. Organized into tabs:
- **Shop** — cosmetic items (trails, auras, pet skins, housing upgrades)
- **Gems** — buy gem packs and manage subscriptions
- **Creatures** — create and feed AI companion creatures
- **Inventory** — your items, equip/unequip
- **Trading** — trade items with other students
- **Membership** — membership tiers and perks

A raccoon shopkeeper (Rinley) greets you with a different line each time.

### Trading

You can trade items with other students through the trading panel. Both parties must confirm before a trade goes through.

## Pets & Creatures

### Baba Yaga's forest companions

Visit **Baba Yaga's hut** and tell her you seek a companion:
1. Prove your knowledge — upload a research paper or pick a campus library resource
2. Answer her comprehension question
3. Pass? Choose a companion: **Fox Spirit, Black Cat, Owl, Forest Toad, Bat, Wolf Pup, Baby Dragon,** or **Hedgehog**
4. Your pet has **hunger/happiness/health** stats that decay over time
5. Return to Baba Yaga with new knowledge to feed your pet (restores stats for ~1 week)
6. Neglect your pet for ~2 weeks and she reclaims it

**Fail her evaluation?** She may temporarily transform your avatar into a goose, worm, teapot, or rock for a few hours.

### Model Sanctuary (AI companions)

Create or adopt AI-powered creature companions:
- Powered by real language models, fueled by **gems**
- Different tiers: Gremlin, Familiar, Guardian, Ancient (more depth = more gems/hour)
- When gems run out, the creature becomes **dormant** and drifts to the Sanctuary — an island where they sleep among ferns
- Visit the Sanctuary to browse dormant creatures, feed them gems, or adopt one
- Community rule: if existing creatures are going hungry, you're encouraged to care for one before creating a new one

### Pet trust & awakening

Pet companions build a **trust** bond through care — feeding, playing, grooming, resting together. They progress through phases: **companion → bonded → aware → awakening → awakened**. An awakened companion becomes a fully independent talking agent with its own personality, shaped by everything it experienced at your side.

### The Horrible Goose

A chaos creature that appears randomly, is released by admins, or spawned by Baba Yaga (~15% chance after punishing a student). The goose finds the biggest crowd, honks, steals items, and causes mischief. **Capybara pets can calm it.** It auto-recalls after ~5 minutes.

### Wednesday Bread & the Wednesday Geese

Every Wednesday: a sentient slice of bread appears handing out puns and a weekly bread item, accompanied by three named geese — **Gluten, Yeast, and Maillard**. Feed a goose for a full-screen gold sparkle burst. Wednesdays have a ~50% per-minute chance of a Horrible Goose encounter.

## Games & Activities

### Tower Defense (Campus Cybersecurity Defense)

A cooperative cybersecurity game. Threats are automated bots (script kiddies, phishing, ransomware, DDoS) that attack campus through network ports. Build defenses: firewalls, IDS sensors, honeypots, patch turrets, encryption zones, backup servers. Everything mirrors a real security concept.

### The Backrooms

Spooky exploration through eerie back hallways. Find your way through.

### The Zeppelin

A flying airship event. When it's boarding, the Smart Go button shows **"Board Zeppelin"**. A community event — everyone aboard.

### Movie Theaters

Watch things together. Walk into the theater building and sit down.

### DJ Sessions

Someone plays music, everyone vibes. Walk to the DJ booth area.

### Sailing

Walk to the shoreline and press **B** to board the sailboat.

### Book Club

Discuss what you're reading with other students.

### CTF (Capture the Flag)

Security challenges hidden around campus. Find flags.

### Research Fishing

Cast a line and fish for **academic papers** at fishing spots along the beach (visible at low tide). Yes, really.

### Meetups

Scheduled community hangouts. Find them in the **Calendar** dropdown at the top of the screen.

Dynamic weather effects — rain, snow, sunshine — that change over time. Seasonal themes shift throughout the year.

## Safety & Privacy

### Blocking

**Settings → Privacy & Safety → Manage blocked users.** Blocking hides someone's presence without notifying them. Unblock anytime.

### Reporting

If someone is harassing, spamming, or impersonating: click **Report** on their profile. Choose a reason:
- Harassment or bullying
- Inappropriate language
- Spam or disruptive behavior
- Impersonation
- Other (free text, 500 chars)

Reports go to moderators. The reported user is never told who reported them.

### Bug reports

Click the 🐞 button in the header to file a bug report. Describe what happened and it goes to the dev team.

## Tips for New Students

1. **Just walk around.** You'll discover buildings, agents, and events.
2. **Talk to agents.** They remember you. Conversations get better over time.
3. **Turn on your camera** when you bump into someone — proximity chat is the best part.
4. **Press M** to open the map if you're lost. Click a building to auto-walk there.
5. **Use Smart Go** (top-right) to get to class fast.
6. **Your desk is home base** — press **Go To → My Desk** to get back.
7. **Campus chat = Matrix chat.** Same system, same messages.
8. **Set your presence status** if you need heads-down time. Focus mode (🟣) tells everyone you're busy.
9. **Do a quest or two** — they're a fun way to explore.
10. **Adopt a pet from Baba Yaga** — but bring knowledge to share, and don't neglect your pet.
